86| TrAIner App 87|

88|

Personal training · AI movement analysis · 20 active users

89| 90|

91| A full-stack personal training application built around AI-powered movement analysis. 92| TrAIner uses pose detection and dynamic time warping to assess exercise technique in real time, 93| comparing each rep against professional reference executions. Currently deployed to 20 users, 94| with a public release planned for both the App Store and Google Play 95| in the future. 96|

97|
98| 99| 100|
101|

102| 103| AI Movement Analysis Pipeline 104|

105|
106|
107|
108|

109| 01 High-Fidelity Estimation 110|

111|

112| Utilizes a top-down pipeline: YOLOv11 for person detection followed by SynthPose (ViTPose-Huge) for extracting 50+ high-precision anatomical keypoints. An IoUTracker maintains identity across frames to isolate the athlete. 113|

114|
115|
116|

117| 02 Temporal Alignment (DTW) 118|

119|

120| Employs Dynamic Time Warping (DTW) to compare user reps against professional references. This allows for accurate technique scoring regardless of the repetition speed, focusing on the biomechanical path. 121|

154| ◈ Routine & Session Management 155|

156|

157| Create custom workout routines with per-exercise set structure: weight ranges, rep ranges, 158| RIR (Reps In Reserve), RPE, and rest timers. Track each session live — the app logs what you 159| actually lifted versus what was planned, with full support for unilateral exercises 160| (left/right side independently). 161|

162|
163| 164|
165|

166| ◈ Progression Tracking & 1RM 167|

168|

169| Automatically calculates your estimated 1-rep max (1RM) from session data. Tracks personal 170| records with visual indicators and maintains a full performance history so you can see 171| exactly how your strength evolves over time. 172|

173|
174| 175|
176|

177| ◈ Social Layer 178|

179|

180| Follow other users, browse a social feed of their sessions, and share routines. 181| Includes a follow-request system for private profiles, session likes, and 182| user search — the training equivalent of a social fitness network. 183|
