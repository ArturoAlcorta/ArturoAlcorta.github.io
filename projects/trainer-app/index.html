TrAIner App

Personal training · AI movement analysis · 20 active users

A full-stack personal training application built around AI-powered movement analysis. TrAIner uses pose detection and dynamic time warping to assess exercise technique in real time, comparing each rep against professional reference executions. Currently deployed to 20 users, with a public release planned for both the App Store and Google Play in the future.

AI Movement Analysis

The core differentiator of TrAIner is its movement analysis pipeline. Using a YOLO11 pose detection model, the app extracts skeleton keypoints from exercise videos in real time. These keypoint sequences are then compared against professional reference executions using Dynamic Time Warping (DTW) — an algorithm well suited for aligning time-series data of different lengths and speeds. The result is a technique score and targeted feedback, regardless of whether you lift slow or fast.

Features

◈ Routine & Session Management

Create custom workout routines with per-exercise set structure: weight ranges, rep ranges, RIR (Reps In Reserve), RPE, and rest timers. Track each session live — the app logs what you actually lifted versus what was planned, with full support for unilateral exercises (left/right side independently).

◈ Progression Tracking & 1RM

Automatically calculates your estimated 1-rep max (1RM) from session data. Tracks personal records with visual indicators and maintains a full performance history so you can see exactly how your strength evolves over time.

◈ Social Layer

Follow other users, browse a social feed of their sessions, and share routines. Includes a follow-request system for private profiles, session likes, and user search — the training equivalent of a social fitness network.

In development

◈ AI Post-session Agent

An AI agent that reviews your completed session data — volume, intensity, technique scores — and generates personalised progression recommendations for your next workout.
