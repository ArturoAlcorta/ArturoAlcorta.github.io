AI Applied to Training

Computer vision and biomechanics turned into something a lifter can act on. It began as one app; two of its pieces have since become projects in their own right.

TrAIner App

AI-powered personal training app with pose detection and Dynamic Time Warping for real-time movement analysis. Now publicly available on the App Store and Google Play.

Gym Technique Analyzer

Standalone version of trAIner's CV engine. Breaks a set of squats, RDLs or bench press into per-rep biomechanics — spine flexion, knee cave, hip-knee coordination, L/R symmetry — from 52-keypoint pose estimation, with an optional DTW comparison against reference lifters.

VBT Tracker

Velocity-based training from a phone video. A YOLO segmentation model tracks the weight plate — whose 45 cm diameter doubles as the pixel-to-metre scale — to measure bar speed, phase timings and estimated RIR per repetition, replacing a several-hundred-euro linear encoder.
