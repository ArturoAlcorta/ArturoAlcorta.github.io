Personal Projects

Two threads run through most of what I build. One is AI applied to training — pose estimation, biomechanics and video pipelines that turn a phone clip into numbers a lifter can act on. The other is generative and semi-supervised computer vision, which grew out of my Masters thesis: using diffusion models to manufacture the training data perception models are always short of. What they share is the part I enjoy most — getting a model out of a notebook and behind an API somebody can actually use. The one below is where the first thread started, and where most of my time still goes; the rest are one click away.
