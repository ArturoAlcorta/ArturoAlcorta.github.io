Rotational spectrum and theoretical calculations of pyrazole⋯CO2 complex: Tetrel and hydrogen bond interactions

Summary: In this research, we utilized Supervised Machine Learning to predict the Potential Energy Surface and interaction energies of the pyrazole-CO2 complex. By training various models on a dataset of 1,258 points, we identified Gaussian Process Regression (GPR) as the top performer, achieving a high accuracy of R2 = 0.993 and an overall RMSD of 1.7 kJ/mol. Notably, the model reached an exceptional precision of 0.52 kJ/mol for stable configurations, effectively mimicking high-level CCSD(T) theoretical calculations.

A Computational Study of the NMR Chemical Shifts of Polynitropyrazoles

Summary: In this research, we applied Machine Learning techniques, specifically Free-Wilson matrices and Extreme Gradient Boosting (XGB) algorithms, to predict NMR (Nuclear Magnetic Resonance) chemical shifts. By integrating these models with GIAO computational methods, we achieved an accuracy of R^2 = 0.999 , successfully modeling how molecular torsion and hydrogen bonding influence the spectroscopic signals of complex nuclei such as 15N and 17O.

Publishing date: 20/Oct/2025
